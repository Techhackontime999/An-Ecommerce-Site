Our Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have to keep your data safe.

Last updated: August 1, 2026 Effective immediately

1. Information we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide our services. Depending on how you use Shop-Seed, this may include:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Delivery information such as your shipping address and postal code.
  • Order history, wishlist, reviews, and payment status (payment itself is processed by secure third-party gateways).
  • Device and usage data such as browser type, pages visited, and preferences like language, currency, and theme.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Process and deliver your orders, including arranging shipping and updates.
  • Personalise your experience with themes, language, and currency preferences.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • Send order confirmations, service notifications, and — only with your consent — promotional offers.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and secure our platform.

3. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal information. We share it only when necessary with:

  • Delivery partners to ship your orders to you.
  • Payment providers to securely process transactions.
  • Service providers who help us run the platform, under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your shopping cart, and remember your preferences such as theme and language. You can clear or block these in your browser settings at any time.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and restricted access controls to protect your data. While no method is 100% secure, we work hard to keep your information safe.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, update, or correct your personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also reach us any time via the contact page.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your data, email us at support@shopseed.com or visit our contact page.
